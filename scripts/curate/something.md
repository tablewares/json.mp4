Here is a 5-scene animated concept designed to visually communicate the **"AI Wealth Effect"** and the growing financial divide:

---

### **Scene 1: The Dual Realities**

* **Primary Objects:**
* **Interactive Object A:** A glowing blue digital network lattice (representing the AI tech boom).
* **Interactive Object B:** A glass floor/ceiling separating two distinct layers.
* **Interactive Object C:** High-rise skyscraper silhouettes on top; modest urban neighborhood storefronts below.


* **Object Interactions & Scene Meaning:**
* The **blue digital lattice** expands upward like roots, connecting directly into the skyscrapers above the glass floor, causing them to pulse with golden light and grow taller.
* Meanwhile, the lattice completely bypasses the neighborhood below, casting a dark shadow over the lower street.
* **Conveyed Idea:** The AI boom operates within a closed loop, elevating top-tier capital centers while completely bypassing the broader real economy beneath it.



---

### **Scene 2: The Stock Rally Ticker**

* **Primary Objects:**
* **Interactive Object A:** A massive, illuminated Wall Street stock ticker chart running upward.
* **Interactive Object B:** A balance scale with a heavy portfolio on one side and a light paycheck on the other.
* **Interactive Object C:** Golden coins and digital code streams.


* **Object Interactions & Scene Meaning:**
* As the **ticker graph spikes upward**, it shoots out streams of **golden coins and digital code**.
* These streams pour heavily into the portfolio side of the **balance scale**, tilting it violently downward and catapulting the small paycheck side high into the air.
* **Conveyed Idea:** Wall Street gains driven by mega-cap tech directly amplify asset wealth, heavily tipping financial leverage away from fixed labor income.



---

### **Scene 3: Divergent Spending Paths**

* **Primary Objects:**
* **Interactive Object A:** A luxury jet/yacht lifting off on a rising escalator made of stock charts.
* **Interactive Object B:** A household shopping cart pushing along a flat, cracked conveyor belt.
* **Interactive Object C:** An inflated red balloon labeled "Inflation" hovering over the conveyor belt.


* **Object Interactions & Scene Meaning:**
* The **luxury jet** smoothly glides up the stock chart escalator, effortlessly clearing the red inflation balloon.
* Concurrently, the **shopping cart** on the ground collides with the **inflation balloon**, which squeezes the cart, forcing items out and slowing its progress to a crawl.
* **Conveyed Idea:** The "wealth effect" shields affluent asset holders from inflation, while everyday wage earners experience shrinking purchasing power.



---

### **Scene 4: The Shrinking Slice (Labor Share)**

* **Primary Objects:**
* **Interactive Object A:** A large, glowing golden pie chart representing total national income.
* **Interactive Object B:** Robotic/AI mechanical arms.
* **Interactive Object C:** A modest wooden serving spatula held by a hand labeled "Labor".


* **Object Interactions & Scene Meaning:**
* The **mechanical arms** swiftly carve out a massive, expanding 90% chunk of the **golden pie chart** and slide it toward corporate/investor vaults.
* The **wooden spatula** reaches in for the remaining piece, but as the mechanical arms expand, the remaining slice shrinks down to a tiny sliver.
* **Conveyed Idea:** As corporate capital and automation capture a larger share of national output, the proportion allocated to worker wages drops to historic lows.



---

### **Scene 5: The Unstable Foundation**

* **Primary Objects:**
* **Interactive Object A:** An inverted pyramid built of golden stock certificates and tech servers balanced on its tip.
* **Interactive Object B:** A foundation floor made of everyday consumer households.
* **Interactive Object C:** Cracks forming in the floor emitting red warning lights.


* **Object Interactions & Scene Meaning:**
* The **inverted pyramid** grows taller and heavier at the top. Its heavy tip presses down onto the **consumer floor**.
* The floor begins to flex under the pressure, causing **cracks emitting red light** to spread outward beneath the pyramid's balance point, making the entire structure wobble.
* **Conveyed Idea:** Extreme wealth concentration built on lagging household incomes creates underlying macroeconomic fragility and market volatility.
